Tuya Light (Local)

Controls a Tuya / Smart Life bulb, strip or dimmer over your LAN, with no cloud in the path. It appears in Control4 as a normal light: on/off, brightness, colour and white temperature.

Setup

  1. Give the bulb a fixed IP (a DHCP reservation on your router).
  2. Get the device's Device ID and Local Key. The easiest way is python -m tinytuya wizard on a computer, with a free Tuya IoT developer project linked to your Smart Life app account. The wizard writes both to devices.json.
  3. Enter Device IP, Device ID and Local Key. Set Protocol Version to what the wizard (or python -m tinytuya scan) reports: 3.3 or 3.4.
  4. Connection Status should go to Online within a few seconds.

Properties

  • Light Type: Auto works it out from what the bulb reports. Override it if a bulb reports datapoints it doesn't really use.
  • DP Profile: Auto, Standard (DPs 20-24, most current bulbs) or Legacy (DPs 1-5). Detected Profile shows what Auto chose.
  • Color Temperature Min/Max (K): the bulb's white range. Most Tuya bulbs are 2700-6500 K.

Notes

  • Re-pairing a device in the Smart Life app changes its Local Key.
  • Many Tuya devices allow only one LAN connection. Close other local integrations (Home Assistant LocalTuya, tinytuya scripts) on the same device.
  • Ramp rates are ignored: Tuya has no transition time on the LAN.
  • Protocols 3.1 and 3.5 are not supported.
