Tuya Bridge

Finds your Tuya / Smart Life devices and hands each Tuya Light or Tuya Plug driver its address and key, so you pick a device from a list instead of typing them in. One bridge per controller.

How it finds devices

  • On the LAN: devices on the controller's network announce themselves every few seconds. The bridge listens (UDP 6666, 6667, 7000) and keeps their addresses current, so a DHCP change follows automatically.
  • Through your Tuya account (optional): with a Tuya IoT project's Access ID and Secret, the bridge fetches every linked account's device names and local keys. Devices on another network, or on protocol 3.5, can then be controlled through the cloud.

Notes

  • Local control never goes through the cloud. The cloud is used for keys and names, and for control only when a device cannot be reached locally.
  • Tuya's free IoT plan limits API calls per month: cloud-controlled devices are polled in one batched request per Cloud Poll Interval.
  • Resource Use shows the bridge's own memory and CPU, refreshed every 5 minutes.
