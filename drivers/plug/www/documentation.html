Tuya Plug (Local)

Controls a Tuya / Smart Life plug, outlet or wall switch over your LAN, with no cloud in the path. It appears in Control4 as an on/off light, so it works with keypads, scenes and programming.

Setup

  1. Give the plug a fixed IP (a DHCP reservation on your router).
  2. Get the device's Device ID and Local Key. The easiest way is python -m tinytuya wizard on a computer, with a free Tuya IoT developer project linked to your Smart Life app account. The wizard writes both to devices.json.
  3. Enter Device IP, Device ID and Local Key, and set Protocol Version (3.3 or 3.4).

Properties

  • Switch DP: 1 for single plugs. Use 2, 3, … for the other gangs of a multi-gang switch.
  • Power Metering: Auto detects metering plugs. Readings show in Power (W), Voltage (V) and Current (mA), and as the variables POWER_W, VOLTAGE_V and CURRENT_MA for programming. They refresh on every poll.

Notes

  • Re-pairing a device in the Smart Life app changes its Local Key.
  • Many Tuya devices allow only one LAN connection, so one driver per device.
  • Protocols 3.1 and 3.5 are not supported.
